CEFR levels and key abilities

C2

Understands virtually everything with ease; summarizes and expresses ideas precisely.

C1

Understands complex texts and implicit meaning; communicates flexibly and effectively.

B2

Understands main ideas of complex texts; communicates fluently and interacts naturally.

B1

Understands familiar topics; handles many situations and describes experiences.

A2

Understands common expressions and simple information; communicates in routine tasks.

A1

Understands basic phrases; introduces oneself and answers simple questions slowly.

Pre-A1

Recognises letters and their sounds; understands and says single everyday words.

Lesson fit: This lesson mainly targets Pre-A1- foundations: letter sounds and everyday words.
